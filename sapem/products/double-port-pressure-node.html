Double Port Pressure Node

$249.99

Monitor two lines from a single unit. The Double Port Pressure Node reads vacuum at two separate points and reports both back to your hub independently, giving you twice the coverage per install — ideal for junctions, mainlines, or anywhere you need to compare pressure across two runs without doubling your hardware.

  • Measures: Vacuum (inHg), two independent ports
  • Reports to: Any SapEM Receiving Hub or Repeater in range
  • Fitting: 5/16" hose barb
  • Range: ~1 mile (line of sight)
  • Battery Life: 2-3 months
