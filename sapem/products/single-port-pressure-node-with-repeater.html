Single Port Pressure Node With Repeater

$249.99

All the monitoring of the standard single-port node, plus a built-in repeater that extends your network's range. Deploy this at the edge of your coverage area and it relays data from nodes further out back to the hub — letting you monitor deep into a sugarbush without a dead zone.

  • Measures: Vacuum (inHg), single port
  • Reports to: Any SapEM Receiving Hub in range
  • Also does: Relays data from other nodes beyond direct hub range
  • Fitting: 5/16" hose barb inlet
  • Range: ~1 mile (line of sight)
  • Battery Life: 2-3 months
  • Best for: Far edges of your sugarbush where you'd otherwise need a separate repeater
