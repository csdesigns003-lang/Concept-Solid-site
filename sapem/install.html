Install the SapEM App

SapEM works like an app on your phone. Add it to your home screen once and you'll get a full-screen icon that opens straight to your dashboard — no browser bars, no typing the address every time.

This only takes about a minute and works on both iPhone and Android — just sign in once first, then add the app to your home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1

Open concept-solid.com/sapem in Safari. (It has to be Safari — Chrome on iPhone can't add to your home screen.)

2

Tap Login in the top right corner and sign in to your SapEM account.

3

Tap the Share icon in the toolbar (the square with an arrow pointing up).

4

Scroll down the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen."

5

Confirm the name and tap "Add" in the top right. The SapEM icon will appear on your home screen.

Double-check: on newer versions of iOS, the "Add to Home Screen" screen shows a toggle or checkbox for opening the site as a web app (sometimes labeled "Open as Website App"). Make sure that's checked — it's what makes SapEM launch full-screen like a real app instead of opening inside Safari.

Android (Chrome)

1

Open concept-solid.com/sapem in Chrome.

2

Tap Login in the top right corner and sign in to your SapEM account.

3

Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner.

4

Tap "Add to Home screen" (sometimes shown as "Install app").

5

Confirm by tapping "Add" or "Install." The SapEM icon will appear on your home screen.

Double-check: if Chrome shows an option to "Open as window" or asks you to confirm it as an app rather than a bookmark, make sure that's selected. That's what makes SapEM launch full-screen like a real app instead of opening a regular browser tab.

Once installed, opening the SapEM icon launches your dashboard directly — you can still log in and check sensors exactly as you would in a browser.
