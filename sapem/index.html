Simple, Affordable Sap System Monitoring

SapEM (Sap Environment Monitoring) Systems develops affordable wireless vacuum monitoring solutions designed specifically for small to medium maple sap operations.

How SapEM Works

Wireless Sensor Nodes

SapEM nodes collect system data and transmit readings over long-range radio communication.

Gateway Hub

The SapEM hub receives sensor data from nodes and forwards readings to the monitoring dashboard.

User Dashboard

Users view sensor data from the connected web app.

Live Dashboard

See Your Sugarbush, Live

Every hub and sensor on one map, with real-time pressure and temperature — all from your phone or laptop.

Interactive Line Map

Every hub and sensor plotted on a satellite map, with your sap tubing drawn right over your actual woods.

System Average Gauge

The main gauge at the top of the dashboard shows your whole system's average vacuum pressure across every sensor.

Low Vacuum Alerts

Sensors running low on vacuum are automatically flagged, so you can spot a leak or line issue before it costs you sap.
